# Shipping and delivery (PayMind Demo Store)

## When your order ships
We start preparing your order as soon as the invoice is paid. Most orders ship within **1 to 2 business days** of payment. The expected delivery date is set when we accept your purchase order and is shown on your order.

## Carriers and tracking
We ship with FedEx, DHL and Blue Dart. When your order ships, we add the carrier and tracking number to your order in the Orders tab, and you can ask the assistant "where is my order?" at any time.

## Delivery confirmation
On the expected delivery date we ask whether your order has arrived. Please confirm it, or tell us if it hasn't: this is how we know an order is complete.

## If your order doesn't arrive
Report it from your order ("Not received"). We check with the carrier within 2 business days and then either ship it again at no cost or refund you in full, whichever you prefer. If tracking shows it was delivered, we may ask you to check with neighbours or your building's reception first.

## Delivery addresses
Put the delivery address and any instructions (reception, floor, contact number) in the notes of your purchase order. We can change the address until the order ships.
