# Disputes, help and contact (PayMind Demo Store)

## Talk to us first
Most problems are solved fastest by messaging us. If you've opened a PayPal dispute, use the message thread on the dispute; otherwise email billing@paymind-demo.example.

## How we handle disputes
Disputes are settled directly between you and us. We reply to every dispute within **2 business days** and before its response date. Depending on the case we'll share tracking details, send you a replacement, or refund you (in full, or part of the price where our refund policy says so). A refund is simply made to you: there's nothing to accept.

## Closing a dispute
Only you can close your dispute. Once we've refunded you or sent a replacement, we'll ask you to confirm: when you're happy, click **✅ Mark as resolved** in the dispute (or tell the assistant). You can also close it any time you're satisfied, for example when a late parcel arrives.

## If you're not happy with what we did
Don't close the dispute: tell us why in its messages. We'll look again, and it stays open until you're satisfied.

## Support hours
Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 India Standard Time. Messages sent outside these hours are answered the next business day.
