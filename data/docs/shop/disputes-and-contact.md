# Disputes, help and contact (PayMind Demo Store)

## Talk to us first
Most problems are solved fastest by messaging us. If you've opened a PayPal dispute, use the message thread on the dispute; otherwise email billing@paymind-demo.example.

## How we handle disputes
We reply to every dispute within **2 business days**, well inside PayPal's response deadline. Depending on the case we'll share tracking or delivery evidence, offer a replacement, or make a refund offer. You can accept or decline an offer from the dispute.

## If we can't agree
Either side can ask PayPal to review the case. PayPal then decides the outcome under its Purchase Protection and Seller Protection programs.

## Support hours
Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 India Standard Time. Messages sent outside these hours are answered the next business day.
