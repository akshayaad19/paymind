# Disputes, help and contact (PayMind Demo Store)

## Talk to us first
Most problems are solved fastest by messaging us. If you've opened a PayPal dispute, use the message thread on the dispute; otherwise email billing@paymind-demo.example.

## How we handle disputes
Disputes are settled directly between you and us. We reply to every dispute within **2 business days** and before its response date. Depending on the case we'll share tracking details, offer a replacement, refund you in full, or offer a partial refund. You can accept or decline an offer by telling the assistant in the chat.

## If you're not happy with our offer
Decline it and tell us why in the dispute's messages. We'll look again and come back with a new proposal, and we don't close a dispute until it's settled with you.

## Support hours
Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 India Standard Time. Messages sent outside these hours are answered the next business day.
