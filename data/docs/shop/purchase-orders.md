# Purchase orders (PayMind Demo Store)

## How to send a purchase order
Open the Orders tab and either upload a photo or scan of your purchase order (handwritten is fine) or type it in. We read the document for you; please check the items, quantities and dates before you send it.

## What to include
Your PO number if you use one, each item with its quantity, the date you need the order by, and delivery instructions. Prices are optional: if you leave them out, we price the order when we accept it.

## Review and acceptance
We review new purchase orders within **1 business day**. If we accept, we confirm the prices and an expected delivery date and send you an invoice that references your PO number. If we can't fulfil it (for example an item is out of stock), we decline it and tell you why.

## Payment terms
Invoices for purchase orders are due within **10 days** (net 10) and are paid through PayPal. Your order is prepared once the invoice is paid.

## Changes and cancellations
You can edit a purchase order until you send it. After that, message us; we can change or cancel it free of charge until it ships.
