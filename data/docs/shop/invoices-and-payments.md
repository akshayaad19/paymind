# Invoices and payments (PayMind Demo Store)

## Paying an invoice
Every invoice can be paid with PayPal from the Invoices tab or from your order. All prices are in US dollars (USD).

## Payment terms
Invoices are due **10 days** after they're issued unless the invoice says otherwise. An invoice shown as "Overdue" has passed its due date; we send reminders for overdue invoices.

## Partial payments
If we've agreed a partial payment, the invoice shows the amount paid so far and the amount still due.

## Receipts and copies
You can download any of your invoices as a PDF at any time from the Invoices tab.

## Payment problems
If a payment fails or you were charged twice, message us with the invoice number and we'll sort it out within 2 business days. Duplicate charges are always refunded in full.
