# Refunds and returns (PayMind Demo Store)

## Return window
You can return most items within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, with all accessories. Consulting hours and other services can't be returned once delivered.

## How to return an item
Message us from your order or dispute, or email billing@paymind-demo.example with your order or invoice number. We reply within 2 business days with a return address and, for faulty items, a prepaid label.

## When you get your money back
Refunds go back to the original PayPal payment. We issue the refund within **5 business days of receiving the return**. PayPal usually shows it in your account straight away; a bank or card can take 3 to 5 more business days.

## Damaged or faulty items
If an item arrives damaged or doesn't work, tell us within 7 days of delivery and include a photo. We send a replacement or a full refund, and you don't have to return the item.

## Partial refunds
We offer a partial refund when an order arrives more than 5 days after the expected delivery date, or when an item is not as described but you'd like to keep it. The amount is agreed with you before it's issued.

## Fees
PayPal doesn't return its transaction fee to us on a refund, but you always get back the full amount you paid.
